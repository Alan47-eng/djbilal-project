Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-22

This policy explains how DJ Bilal Music Store processes user data for accounts, checkout, downloads, and support.

Data we process

Email address, account profile data, and technical logs required for secure service delivery. If you start a checkout, we also process the buyer and billing/shipping details required by the payment provider.

Data usage

Data is used to manage accounts, provide downloads, prevent abuse, and support users. Checkout details are transmitted to Shopier to initiate and process payment. The store records the order, selected tracks, amount, and payment/delivery status; card details are entered and processed by Shopier, not stored by this store.

Payment provider

Shopier processes payment information under its own privacy terms. Please review Shopier's privacy information before completing a payment. We do not receive or store your full payment-card number or security code.
